About Arya Enterprises

Pioneering sustainable energy solutions since 2014. We're committed to powering a greener future through innovative solar and power technologies.

Who We Are

Leading the Way in Sustainable Energy

Arya Enterprises was founded in 2014 with a vision to make clean, renewable energy accessible to everyone. What started as a small venture has grown into one of the most trusted names in the solar and power solutions industry.

We specialize in providing comprehensive energy solutions including solar PV systems, inverters, UPS systems, and batteries. Our team of certified professionals ensures that every installation meets the highest standards of quality and efficiency.

With over 500+ successful installations and 1000+ satisfied customers, we have established ourselves as a reliable partner for both residential and commercial energy needs. Our commitment to excellence and customer satisfaction has been the cornerstone of our success.

Our Journey

A Decade of Excellence

From humble beginnings to industry leadership, here's how we've grown over the years.

2014

Company Founded

Arya Enterprises was established with a vision to bring sustainable energy solutions to the masses. Started with a small team of 5 passionate individuals.

2016

First 100 Installations

Achieved our first major milestone of 100 successful solar installations. Expanded our product range to include inverters and UPS systems.

2018

ISO Certification

Received ISO 9001:2015 certification for our commitment to quality management systems. Team grew to 25 members.

2020

Commercial Expansion

Expanded into commercial and industrial solar solutions. Completed our first 1MW solar plant project.

2024

500+ Projects & Growing

Today, with 500+ successful installations, 50+ team members, and presence across multiple states, we continue to lead the solar revolution.
