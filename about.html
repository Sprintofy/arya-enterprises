About Arya Enterprises

Pioneering India's solar revolution since 2014. We harness the power of the sun to deliver clean, affordable, and sustainable energy solutions for homes, businesses, and industries nationwide.

Powering India's Solar Future

India's Trusted Solar Energy Partner

Arya Enterprises was founded in 2014 with a revolutionary vision: to make solar energy accessible to every Indian household and business. As India receives over 300 days of sunshine annually, we saw the immense potential to transform how our nation powers itself.

We specialize in end-to-end solar solutions including rooftop solar PV systems, ground-mounted solar plants, solar inverters, hybrid energy systems, and advanced battery storage solutions. Our MNRE-approved installations utilize tier-1 solar panels from leading manufacturers like Trina Solar, Jinko Solar, and Adani Solar.

With over 15 MW of cumulative solar capacity installed across 500+ residential, commercial, and industrial projects, we've helped our customers save crores in electricity bills while reducing over 10,000 tonnes of CO2 emissions annually. Our commitment to quality, backed by 25-year performance warranties, has made us a preferred solar partner across North India.

Our Solar Journey

A Decade of Solar Excellence

From our first 5 kW installation to megawatt-scale solar plants—tracing our growth in India's solar revolution.

2014

Solar Seed Planted

Founded Arya Enterprises with a mission to bring solar energy to Indian homes. Installed our first 5 kW residential rooftop system in Delhi NCR, generating 7,500 units annually.

2016

Commercial Solar Expansion

Crossed 100 residential installations. Entered commercial solar market with our first 100 kW factory rooftop project. Became authorized dealers for Luminous, Microtek, and Havells inverters.

2018

MNRE Empanelment

Achieved MNRE Channel Partner status and ISO 9001:2015 certification. Launched battery storage solutions with lithium-ion technology. Team expanded to 25 solar professionals.

2021

Megawatt Milestone

Commissioned first 1 MW ground-mounted solar plant for industrial client. Crossed 5 MW cumulative installation. Launched EV charging solutions integrated with solar.

2024

15 MW & Growing Strong

Today with 15+ MW installed capacity, 500+ projects, 50+ team members, and presence across 5 states, we're a leading solar EPC company supporting PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana.
